One-page newspaper front page summing up the world news of Sunday
22 November 2026: 47 stories from every continent, each with its own
flat-cartoon illustration, drawn by a pure Python standard-library program.

## The day in the image

- **Iran hits US bases in Bahrain** — IRGC missiles and drones strike
  Patriot radars; Bahrain says a plant was hit.
- **COP31 draft drops fossil fuels** — the Antalya text erases coal, oil
  and gas and admits a 1.5C overshoot.
- **Peru quake M6.7** — the Andes tremor sends Lima residents into the
  streets; no casualties reported.
- Plus the Sparta II fire in the Gulf of Finland, the Kryvyi Rih
  triple-tap strike, the capture of El Chapo Isidro in Sinaloa, Nepal's
  PM landing in Delhi, and 40 more stories across politics, climate,
  sport and culture.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Writes `out/entry.png` (1024x1024). Pure stdlib, no network, deterministic:
two runs write byte-identical files.
